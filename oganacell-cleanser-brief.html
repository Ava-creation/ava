01 — The Pitch
It foams up on its own. You don't scrub, you don't lather — you just wait.

The gel uses an amino-acid surfactant system paired with Oganacell's Aero Energy™ bubble technology. Apply on dry skin and within ~30 seconds it builds a dense, cushiony oxygen foam that lifts away surface oil, light makeup, and SPF residue.

Position it like this: a gentle-but-deep daily cleanser for sensitive, post-procedure, and oily-sensitive (dehydrated-but-oily) skin. Not a makeup remover, not an exfoliant — a cleanser that feels like treatment.

09 — Compliance

Say this, not that.

  • "It foams up on its own. No water, no rubbing."

  • "After one use in testing, results showed less oil, less dust, less redness."

Don't say

  • Show pore changes after a single wash. Pores need 14/28-day by-day footage.
